1. Macro Metrics & Indicators Interpretation

  • Federal Reserve Net Liquidity ($Reserves = WALCL - TGA - RRP$):
    How to interpret: There is a direct relationship between central bank bank reserves and asset valuations. When Net Liquidity expands, capital flows into risk assets (driving SPY higher). When it drops, structural market support collapses.
    Action: If SPY rises while Net Liquidity drops (Bearish Divergence), hedge or close swing positions. If Liquidity rises while SPY is flat, prepare for a swing breakout.
  • VIX / VXV Term Structure:
    How to interpret: The ratio of front-month VIX to 3-month VXV.
    Contango (Ratio < 1.0): Risk-on. Front month fear is low. Market is quiet. Excellent for selling premium or holding swing longs.
    Backwardation (Ratio ≥ 1.0): Risk-off. Front month fear is higher than back month. Extreme panic. Stay flat or hedge.
  • SKEW Index:
    How to interpret: Measures institutional demand for extreme out-of-the-money protection puts.
    Normal (< 125): Balanced tail risk.
    Extreme (≥ 140): Outlier hedge stacking. Institutions are pricing in black swan risk. Expect sharp correction drops.
  • OPEX Calendar (Option Expirations):
    How to interpret: Days remaining until the monthly Friday options expiration (3rd Friday).
    Unwinding Effect: As days left < 5, market makers close/roll positions. Under Positive Gamma, this forces prices to "pin" close to major Call/Put Walls. Under Negative Gamma, it triggers Charm/Vanna delta squeezes.
  • Relative Strength (RS) Leaderboard:
    How to interpret: Calculated daily as `Stock Performance (%) - SPY Performance (%)`.
    Action: On bullish market days ($ADD > +500$), trade breakouts ONLY on the top 2 RS leaders (strongest momentum tailwind). On bearish days, short the bottom 2 RS laggards.

2. Executable Trading Setups (Entry, Stop, Target)

Setup A: Range Breakout / Wall Breach
Logic: Price closes above a 20-day high (excluding today) on heavy volume (≥ 1.5x average), OR breaches above/below the major dealer Call/Put Wall.
Entry: Buy stop-limit order placed 1 tick above the breakout candle high.
Stop Loss: 1 tick below the breakout candle low (for immediate invalidation).
Profit Target: The next major GEX wall strike, or a trailing stop set at a 2.0x risk multiple.
Setup B: VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern)
Logic: Price undergoes 2 to 4 contraction waves where swing depth decreases sequentially (e.g. 15% → 8% → 4%) while volume dries up dramatically (< 0.85x average).
Entry: Buy the pivot high breach when buying volume surges, confirmed by stacked footprint imbalances (≥ 3.5x bid/ask).
Stop Loss: 1 tick below the lowest point of the final contraction wave (the "cheat" pivot low).
Profit Target: Previous major swing highs or the underlying Call Wall strike.
Setup C: Trend Continuation (EMA Pullback)
Logic: In a strong trend (20 EMA > 50 EMA), price pulls back to test the 20-day EMA on light volume (current volume < 20-day average).
Entry: Buy when price holds the 20-day EMA and prints a bullish reversal candlestick wick on the footprint chart.
Stop Loss: 1 tick below the 20-day EMA, or below the pullback bar low.
Profit Target: Preceding swing high, or the next major overhead Call Wall strike.
Setup D: Mean Reversion (Wall Exhaustion)
Logic: Spot price trades within 0.5% of the major Call Wall or Put Wall, or spot price is overextended (> 5%) from its 20-day EMA.
Entry: Limit order placed at the Wall, or a market order triggered by a reversal footprint pattern (e.g. Hammer at Put Wall) with MLOFI order book support.
Stop Loss: 1 tick beyond the Wall strike or 1 tick beyond the wick/tail of the reversal candle.
Profit Target: The GEX Flip Level (which acts as the center of gravity/reversion anchor).
Setup E: Unusual Options Volume Spike
Logic: Current shares volume is ≥ 2.0x average volume, OR options unusual activity (UOA) sweep ratio is ≥ 1.5x of open interest.
Entry: Enter in the direction of the options sweep (Calls for long, Puts for short) once order flow speedometer needle pivots hard to the green/red zone.
Stop Loss: Below the Point of Control (POC) price level of the breakout footprint bar.
Profit Target: Intraday GEX boundaries (dealer gamma hedging halts the momentum).
