Gamma GEX Strategy Blueprint & Cheat Sheet

A comprehensive breakdown of how options market maker hedging guides price action

1. The Core GEX Volatility Regime Principle

Market maker hedging actions dictate realized market volatility based on the sign of dealer GEX:

  • Positive Gamma Regime (Spot Price > Flip Level): Market makers buy stock as it drops and sell stock as it rises to keep delta-neutral. This dampens market movement. Best Strategies: Iron Condors, Range-Bound Mean Reversion, Selling Premium.
  • Negative Gamma Regime (Spot Price < Flip Level): Market makers sell stock as it drops and buy stock as it rises to keep delta-neutral. This accelerates market movement. Best Strategies: Long Straddles/Strangles, Breakout Trend Following, Protective Put Buying.

2. Option Volume Imbalance (OVI) Breakouts

Open interest is static (updated once per day), but volume shows intraday institutional commitments. The Option Volume Imbalance measures the ratio of call vs put volume:

  • Bullish breakout confirmation: When spot price breaks resistance AND OVI is high (> 0.3), institutions are aggressively bidding call options. Dealers are forced to buy stock immediately, fueling a breakout.
  • Bearish breakout confirmation: When spot price breaks support AND OVI is low (< -0.3), institutions are bidding put options. Dealers are forced to short stock immediately, fueling a drop.

3. Gamma vs. Time (Overextension & OPEX Reversals)

Option expiration (OPEX) weeks amplify dealer hedging behavior due to **Charm** (delta decay over time). As expiration approaches ($T \to 0$):

  • Call Wall Reversals: If spot is overextended upwards towards the Call Wall, dealer buying halts once they are fully delta-hedged. If the price stalls, decay (Charm) forces dealers to sell their stock hedge, creating a swift reversal down.
  • Put Wall Reversals: If spot drops to the Put Wall, dealer selling ceases. A slight bounce in spot forces dealers to quickly cover their short hedges, triggering an OPEX short-covering bounce.

4. The Confluence Framework: GEX + Order Flow + Price Action

To trade with institutional precision, we combine three layers of market metrics:

  • GEX Levels (The Map): Identifies the key structural price areas where price action is likely to stall or accelerate (Call Wall, Put Wall, GEX Flip level, and Max Gamma Strike).
  • Order Flow (The Engine): Monitors real-time transaction dynamics at these boundaries. Tracks resting depth shifts (MLOFI), execution speed (speedometer dial), passive support (Center of Gravity paths), and buying/selling exhaustion (Sonar Pulse alerts).
  • Price Action (The Trigger): Uses footprint bar candlestick patterns (Hammer at Put Wall, Shooting Star at Call Wall, Marubozu breakouts at the Flip) to trigger entries and define precise stop-losses (e.g. Stop-loss placed just 1 tick behind the hammer wick).
